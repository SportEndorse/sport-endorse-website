Unrivaled: The 3x3 Women's Basketball League Redefining the Game

How Unrivaled — the 3x3 women's basketball league co-founded by Breanna Stewart and Napheesa Collier — is reshaping the sport through equity, viewership and sponsorship.

By Sport Endorse Team, Sport Endorse · 2025-04-10 · 4 min read

Unrivaled, the 3x3 women's basketball league co-founded by WNBA stars Breanna Stewart and Napheesa Collier, is reshaping the women's game — putting player equity first, drawing record viewership and attracting major sponsors. Here's why it matters for the sport, and for brands watching women's sport rise.

A New Era in Women’s Basketball

Unrivaled distinguishes itself with a three-on-three format, offering a fast-paced and engaging experience for both players and fans. This structure not only showcases the athletes’ versatility but also aligns with the growing global popularity of 3×3 basketball. By focusing on this format, Unrivaled taps into a dynamic style of play that resonates with contemporary audiences, making the game more accessible and commercially viable.

Player Empowerment at the Forefront

Central to Unrivaled’s mission is the empowerment of its athletes. The league has implemented a player-centric model that includes competitive compensation and equity stakes, ensuring that players benefit directly from the league’s success. This approach addresses longstanding issues in women’s sports regarding fair pay and representation, setting a new standard for professional leagues. Platforms like Sport Endorse, which specialize in connecting athletes with brands through equitable partnerships, underscore the broader shift toward athlete-driven innovation in sports. By prioritizing authenticity and mutual value, such platforms align with Unrivaled’s mission to redefine ownership and representation in women’s basketball, ensuring athletes remain central to the evolution of the game.

Viewership Success and Digital Engagement

Unrivaled’s collaboration with TNT Sports has been instrumental in amplifying its reach. The inaugural season averaged 221,000 viewers across broadcasts on TNT and truTV, with the debut games attracting over 300,000 viewers each. These figures not only surpass typical viewership for women’s basketball on cable but also highlight the growing appetite for women’s sports content.

Beyond traditional broadcasts, Unrivaled generated an astonishing 589 million earned and owned social media impressions, reaching more than 11.9 million total viewers during its first season. This robust digital presence underscores the league’s ability to connect with younger, digitally engaged audiences and leverage social media as a key driver of brand engagement and sponsorship value.

A Game-Changer for Corporate Partnerships

The league’s business model has been further validated by the impressive array of 20+ corporate partners it secured during its inaugural season. Since announcing Ally Financial as its founding partner in mid-July, Unrivaled has attracted sponsorships from some of the most notable brands committed to advancing women’s sports, including:

  • Technology & Media: Samsung Galaxy, TNT Sports, Mediapro North America
  • Retail & Apparel: Sephora U.S., Wayfair, Under Armour
  • Financial & Healthcare: Morgan Stanley, Mount Sinai Medical Center, PwC, Opill
  • Beverage & Consumer Goods: Miller Lite, BODYARMOR, Sprite, Icy Hot
  • Sports & Gaming: Wilson, DraftKings, Ticketmaster
  • Marketing & Insurance: VistaPrint, State Farm, Adobe

These partnerships not only provide financial support but also enhance the league’s visibility and fan engagement through innovative content creation and co-branded activations. The level of investment from these high-profile sponsors signals a turning point for women’s professional sports, demonstrating that brands recognize the immense value and potential of this market.

Implications for the Broader Sports Industry

Unrivaled’s success offers valuable insights for the sports industry at large. By prioritizing player empowerment, embracing innovative formats, and leveraging strategic partnerships, the league presents a replicable model for other sports organizations aiming to foster growth and engagement. The emphasis on equitable treatment and active promotion of athletes sets a precedent that could influence future developments in professional sports, particularly in addressing the long-standing disparities between men’s and women’s leagues.

A Transformative Force in Women’s Basketball

In conclusion, Unrivaled is not merely a new entrant in women’s basketball—it is a transformative force reshaping the dynamics of the sport. Through its commitment to innovation, player empowerment, and strategic collaboration, Unrivaled exemplifies how visionary leadership can drive meaningful change in the sports industry. With growing fan interest, strong corporate backing, and a game-changing approach to player equity, Unrivaled is setting a new standard for professional basketball and beyond.
