After years of watching ambassador deals succeed and fail, a pattern is obvious: the deals that go wrong were almost always under-specified at signature. Here's the checklist we'd want every brand and athlete to work through before signing — nine clauses that prevent ninety percent of disputes.

## 1. Deliverables, counted

"Regular social content" is not a deliverable. "Eight Instagram feed posts, four stories per month, two appearance days per year" is. Count everything, including revisions.

## 2. Term and renewal

Start and end dates, plus what happens at the end — automatic renewal, renegotiation, or clean exit. Ambiguity here is where relationships sour.

## 3. Exclusivity, scoped

Category exclusivity ("no other sports nutrition brands") is normal; total exclusivity is expensive and usually unnecessary. Define the category precisely — "beverages" and "energy drinks" are very different scopes with very different price tags.

## 4. Usage rights

Who can reuse the content, where, and for how long? Organic social reuse, paid amplification, out-of-home, packaging — each is a separate right. Buying only what you'll use keeps fees sensible; discovering you need paid rights after a post over-performs is the expensive way to learn this.

## 5. Approval workflow

Who signs off content before it goes live, and how fast? In regulated categories — healthcare, finance, alcohol — this clause is the difference between a campaign and a compliance incident. Document the approvals.

## 6. Disclosure

Sponsored content must be disclosed — #ad and platform-native tools, per ASAI guidance in Ireland, the ASA/CMA in the UK, and the FTC in the US. Put the obligation in the agreement so nobody is improvising.

## 7. Payment terms

Amounts, schedule, and what triggers payment. Milestone-based payment against completed deliverables protects both sides — which is exactly how in-platform deals on Sport Endorse are structured.

## 8. Conduct and exit

A mutual standard: what happens if either party's conduct damages the other's reputation, and how the agreement unwinds — including what happens to content already published.

## 9. Measurement

Agree what success looks like before launch: reach, engagement, code redemptions, appearances completed. If it's measured from day one, the renewal conversation writes itself.

---

None of this needs to make deals slower. On a platform, most of these clauses are structured fields agreed in minutes rather than bespoke legal drafting — which is rather the point. Specificity up front is what makes partnerships fast, not slow.
