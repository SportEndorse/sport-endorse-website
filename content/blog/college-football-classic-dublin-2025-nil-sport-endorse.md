## **College Football Classic Dublin 2025: Kansas State vs. Iowa State and the NIL Global Stage**

On **Saturday, August 23, 2025**, Dublin’s Aviva Stadium will host a historic *Big 12* showdown between the **Kansas State Wildcats** and the **Iowa State Cyclones**. It’s more than just a football game—it’s a transatlantic sports and business opportunity where **Name, Image, and Likeness (NIL)** rights turn student-athletes into global brand ambassadors.

With **22,000 U.S. fans expected** to travel and a projected **€130+ million boost** to the Irish economy, the [Aer Lingus College Football Classic](https://collegefootballireland.com) will be broadcast live on ESPN to millions back home. It’s a chance for American college athletes to play on European soil—and for brands to tap into both markets at once.

### **The NIL Market: A $2.55 Billion Wave**

Since the NCAA lifted restrictions in 2021, NIL deals have transformed college sports marketing. According to [**EMarketer** ](https://articles-na1.emarketer.com/)(March 2025), the total NIL market for the upcoming sports year is projected at **$2.55 billion**, with around **$500 million directly tied to marketing campaigns**. But that’s just the surface—collectives and donors often channel additional marketing dollars into NIL activities, making the real brand opportunity even bigger.

**Category insight matters.** While *automotive* accounts for only ~1% of NIL deal volume, it represents nearly **one-third of total NIL spend**, often via luxury vehicle partnerships for star players. *Retail*, on the other hand, dominates in volume but tends to focus on low-cost activations, like quick-turn social posts. Two-thirds of NIL social deals cost under **$1,000**, making them accessible for even small Dublin-based businesses during game week
