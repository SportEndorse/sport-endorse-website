Women's sports are one of the most under-tapped opportunities in marketing: a fast-growing audience, rising sponsorship value and expanding global reach. Brands that invest early in female athletes stand to gain outsized returns. Here's where the growth is, and how businesses can capture it.

### 1. Market Potential: Exploring the Untapped Market of Women’s Sports

The market for women’s sports is vast and largely untapped, offering a wealth of opportunities for businesses to engage with a passionate and growing audience The market for women’s sports is rapidly growing. It is anticipated that 2024 will be the year when the global women’s sports industry [surpasses the $1 billion mark, representing a 300% increase from 2021.](https://www.nielsen.com/insights/2024/whats-next-for-womens-sports-fueling-growth-proving-value/)

Recent trends indicate that interest in women’s sports is increasing, with notable growth in areas such as [basketball ](https://www.wnba.com/news/2023-season-record-breaking-success-recap)and [football](https://www.skysports.com/more-sports/news/29877/13065839/womens-sport-draws-record-viewership-in-2023-after-fifa-womens-world-cup-and-solheim-cup-drama#:~:text=The%20FIFA%20Women's%20World%20Cup%20final%2C%20which%20England%20lost%201,with%2038.4%20million%20viewing%20hours.). According to Nielsen’s report, “[Women’s Sports Viewership on the Rise](https://www.nielsen.com/insights/2023/womens-sports-viewership-on-the-rise/)“, there  as been a 42% increase in average viewership for the WNBA in 2023 compared to the previous year. [The FIFA Women’s World Cup 2023 also set new records for global viewership and attendance](https://www.skysports.com/more-sports/news/29877/13065839/womens-sport-draws-record-viewership-in-2023-after-fifa-womens-world-cup-and-solheim-cup-drama#:~:text=The%20FIFA%20Women's%20World%20Cup%20final%2C%20which%20England%20lost%201,with%2038.4%20million%20viewing%20hours.).

By investing in women’s sports, businesses can tap into this growing market and align themselves with equality, empowerment, and perseverance values that resonate deeply with today’s consumers.

### 2. Sponsorship Dynamics: Maximizing Value through Female Athlete Endorsements

Women’s sports sponsorships provide brands with a unique and valuable opportunity to connect with consumers. Female athletes have an inspiring narrative that resonates with various demographics. Names such as Kellie Harrington, Rhasidat Adeleke, Mona McSharry have become household names in Ireland post Paris 2024, generating significant interest and engagement.

According to the [2023 Nielsen Sports report](https://www.nielsen.com/insights/2023/womens-sports-viewership-on-the-rise/), investing in women’s sports delivers strong ROI: 44% of WNBA fans visited a sponsor’s website and 28% made a purchase, compared to 36% and 24% for NBA fans. Moreover, 69% of Women’s World Cup fans find sponsors more appealing, highlighting the significant brand benefits.

### 3. Global Reach: Expanding the Influence of Women’s Sports Internationally

Women’s sports are making a significant impact, globally, breaking down cultural and geographic barriers. The 2022 Women’s EURO became the most-watched edition ever, with a global cumulative live viewership of 365 million, more than double the 178 million from 2017 and 214% higher than the 116 million in 2013 [UEFA Official Report](https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/0278-15ff73f066e1-c729b5099cbb-1000--365-million-people-watch-women-s-euro-2022/). Similarly, the 2023 US Women’s Open saw its highest viewership in nine years, with over 2.2 million peak viewers on NBC [ESPN Press Room](https://espnpressroom.com/us/press-releases/2023/09/coco-gauffs-us-open-victory-marks-most-viewed-womens-major-tennis-final-ever-on-espn/#:~:text=The%202023%20US%20Open%20Men's,ESPN%2C%20behind%20only%202019's%20Championships). These figures highlight the growing global appeal of women’s sports. By supporting, women’s sports events and athletes, organizations build a strong international brand and interact with a diverse audience. This global impact promotes cultural interchange and the concepts of inclusiveness and diversity.
