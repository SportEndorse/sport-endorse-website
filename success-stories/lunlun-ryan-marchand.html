LunLun Talks × Ryan Marchand — Basketball Podcast (Taiwan)

LunLun Talks featured elite coach Ryan Marchand in a cross-market basketball podcast that earned 5,000+ views and strong fan engagement. ->.

Punditry & podcastBasketballInternational

Overview

The campaign

Taiwan's LunLun Talks partnered with Sport Endorse to feature Ryan Marchand, head coach of the New Taipei Kings, in an in-depth basketball podcast. The interview explored coaching philosophy, leadership and his international journey, then reached audiences through YouTube and social media, generating more than 5,000 views and strong fan engagement.

The challenge

LunLun Talks wanted to strengthen its role as a credible platform for influential voices in basketball. To achieve that, the podcast needed more than a familiar guest. It required a coach with genuine authority, an international perspective and enough practical experience to offer listeners useful insight into how elite teams are led, developed and prepared for competition.

The guest also had to be relevant to Taiwan's rapidly growing professional basketball audience. A successful episode would need to appeal to fans and players while giving the conversation enough depth to travel beyond a single news cycle. The central challenge was therefore to identify a basketball figure whose personal journey, leadership approach and professional role could support an authentic long-form discussion, then turn that conversation into content suitable for both YouTube and social distribution.

The Sport Endorse solution

Sport Endorse supported the connection between LunLun Talks and Ryan Marchand, who was serving as head coach of the New Taipei Kings in Taiwan's P. LEAGUE+. His role placed him directly inside the country's professional basketball environment and gave the podcast access to a guest able to discuss the sport from a leadership and coaching perspective rather than through generic commentary.

The resulting activation centred on one in-depth podcast conversation. According to the official Sport Endorse success story, the episode explored Marchand's coaching philosophy, leadership style and international journey. This created a clear editorial structure: listeners could learn how an elite coach thinks, how leadership principles translate into team culture, and how experience across different basketball environments can shape a professional career.

LunLun Talks distributed the interview through YouTube and its social platforms, allowing the long-form episode to become the anchor for wider digital promotion. Sport Endorse's published case study reports more than 5,000 views and strong engagement from fans and players, showing that the subject matter resonated with the intended basketball community. No matching HubSpot Deal was located during the internal review, so campaign dates, production logistics and detailed platform analytics have not been reconstructed or estimated.

Athlete fit

Ryan Marchand was a strong fit because his professional credibility matched the podcast's editorial objective. As head coach of the New Taipei Kings, he could speak from direct experience about decision-making, team development and the demands of leadership in professional basketball. His position gave the conversation substance and made the episode relevant to both committed fans and people involved in the sport.

His international journey added another useful layer. Rather than limiting the discussion to one team or season, LunLun Talks could explore how different experiences informed Marchand's coaching identity and leadership style. That combination of local relevance and broader perspective made the partnership feel natural. The podcast was not borrowing an athlete's visibility for a superficial endorsement; it was using a qualified sporting voice to create informative media with lasting value for Taiwan's basketball community.

Deliverables

  • The available public records confirm the following activation structure and deliverables:
  • One elite basketball coach secured as the featured podcast guest.
  • One in-depth interview focused on coaching philosophy, leadership and an international career journey.
  • Long-form episode distributed through the LunLun Talks YouTube channel.
  • Supporting promotion shared across LunLun Talks social platforms.
  • Content positioned for basketball fans, players and the wider Asian sports-media community.
  • Completed activation published by Sport Endorse as a verified success story.

Results

The strongest verified performance result is more than 5,000 views for the interview, accompanied by strong engagement from fans and players. The evidence also confirms one featured coach, one long-form podcast episode and distribution across YouTube and social media. Precise watch time, completion rate, comments, shares, social impressions, engagement rate and audience-demographic data were not available in the sources reviewed, so none have been estimated.
