Wellness & Nutrition

Sunvit-D3 × Sophie Coldwell — Pregnancy-to-Performance Journey

Sunvit-D3 partnered with Sport Endorse to build authentic maternal-performance brand credibility through professional triathlete Sophie Evans' pregnancy and return to elite competition.

Athlete programmeTriathlonUK

Overview

The campaign

Sunvit-D3 partnered with Sport Endorse to strengthen its position as a trusted performance nutrition brand by connecting directly with professional triathlete Sophie Evans. Managed seamlessly through our digital platform.

The challenge

For health and wellness brands, authenticity is critical. Consumers increasingly expect athlete partnerships to reflect genuine personal experiences rather than traditional product endorsements, particularly when campaigns touch on sensitive areas such as pregnancy, recovery and maternal health.

Sunvit-D3 wanted to demonstrate how its vitamin D supplement could naturally fit into an elite athlete's wellness routine while creating content that would resonate with both performance-focused athletes and active mothers. Finding an athlete whose personal journey aligned so closely with the brand's values required careful matchmaking and trusted industry connections.

The Sport Endorse solution

Sport Endorse introduced Sunvit-D3 to British professional triathlete Sophie Evans (formerly Sophie Coldwell), whose pregnancy and return to elite racing created a unique opportunity for authentic long-term storytelling.

Rather than focusing on a single promotional activation, the partnership centred on documenting Sophie's real-life journey through pregnancy, recovery and her gradual return to world-class competition. Throughout the campaign, Sophie integrated Sunvit-D3 naturally into her wider nutrition and wellness routine while sharing regular updates with her highly engaged Instagram audience.

The campaign was monitored using social performance tracking, allowing Sunvit-D3 to measure engagement, reach, video consumption and earned media value across all published content.

Athlete fit

Sophie Evans was an exceptional ambassador for Sunvit-D3 because she combined elite sporting credibility with a highly relatable personal journey.

As an internationally recognised professional triathlete, Sophie already had strong authority within the endurance sports community. During the campaign she openly documented her experiences throughout pregnancy, postpartum recovery and her return to professional competition, creating genuine educational and inspirational content rather than traditional advertising.

Her audience naturally overlapped with Sunvit-D3's target consumers:

Endurance athletes

Active women

Mothers returning to exercise

Health-conscious consumers

Performance-focused individuals seeking trusted nutritional support

The result was a partnership built around lived experience instead of scripted promotion.

Deliverables

  • Sport Endorse facilitated the partnership between Sunvit-D3 and professional triathlete Sophie Evans.
  • One professional athlete ambassador activated throughout the campaign.
  • 25 pieces of Instagram content published:
  • 13 Reels
  • 10 Stories
  • 2 Feed Posts
  • Authentic pregnancy and postpartum performance storytelling.
  • Ongoing product integration within Sophie's nutrition and training routine.
