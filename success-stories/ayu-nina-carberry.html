AYU Cosmetics × Nina Carberry — Brand Face

Champion jockey Nina Carberry became the new face of cosmetics brand AYU, embodying its values of hard work and determination.

Brand ambassadorEquestrianIreland

Overview

The campaign

AYU sought a new face for its popular cosmetics and, via Sport Endorse, selected champion jockey Nina Carberry. Nina embodies AYU's values of hard work, loyalty and determination, showing what can be achieved through dedication.

“Nina personifies what the AYU brand is all about — that dreams come true if we have the courage to pursue them.”— Suzie O'Neill, Owner, AYU
