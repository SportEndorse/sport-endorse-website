Wellness & Nutrition

Sons × GAA & Rugby Talent — Grassroots Men's Health

Sport Endorse helped Sons activate trusted GAA ambassadors through authentic creator content, delivering measurable engagement and reusable brand assets with seamless campaign management.

Brand ambassadorGAAUK

Overview

The campaign

Sons partnered with Sport Endorse to build brand awareness among Ireland's sporting community by connecting directly with trusted GAA ambassadors including Paudie Clifford, Lee Keegan and Jordan Flynn. Managed seamlessly through our digital platform.

The challenge

Sons, one of Ireland's leading men's hair health brands, wanted to strengthen awareness within the country's sporting community by partnering with authentic Gaelic footballers whose audiences aligned naturally with the brand's mission.

Rather than relying on polished influencer marketing, Sons wanted credible athletes who could genuinely demonstrate how hair care fits into the everyday routine of training, recovery and performance. The challenge extended beyond athlete selection. Managing outreach, evaluating applicants, negotiating agreements, coordinating deliverables and securing long-term content usage rights can quickly become time-consuming when handled manually across multiple creators.

Sons needed an efficient way to identify the right talent, streamline campaign management and ensure all content could be repurposed across both organic and paid marketing channels.

The Sport Endorse solution

Using the Sport Endorse platform, Sons launched a targeted athlete collaboration campaign focused on Irish GAA talent. The platform enabled the team to receive verified athlete applications, compare suitable creators, communicate directly with talent and manage campaign agreements from a single workflow.

Sport Endorse coordinated the end-to-end campaign, including athlete sourcing, campaign administration, creator communication, deliverable management and payment coordination. The platform also ensured that perpetual organic and paid usage rights were secured, allowing Sons to maximise the long-term value of the content beyond the initial campaign.

Alongside campaign management, Sport Endorse supported the brand in activating respected GAA ambassadors including Paudie Clifford, Lee Keegan and Jordan Flynn, helping Sons position hair care as a natural part of an active sporting lifestyle.

Athlete fit

The selected athletes represented an ideal fit for the Sons brand because they embodied the values the campaign sought to communicate: commitment, resilience, consistency and everyday performance.

Unlike traditional lifestyle influencers, these players were recognised primarily for their sporting achievements and trusted voices within Irish communities. Their audiences consisted largely of sports fans, amateur athletes and active men-the exact demographic Sons wanted to reach.

Rather than creating highly polished promotional content, each athlete integrated Sons products naturally into their existing routines, demonstrating how maintaining healthy hair can become part of daily preparation, recovery and self-care without disrupting an athlete's lifestyle. This authenticity helped reinforce Sons' positioning as a practical, science-backed solution designed for active men.

Deliverables

  • 11 verified athlete applications received and reviewed through the Sport Endorse platform
  • 3 GAA ambassadors selected and activated for the campaign
  • 5 pieces of Instagram content delivered (2 Reels, 2 Stories, 1 Feed Post)
  • Perpetual organic and paid social media usage rights secured for all campaign content
  • Athlete contracts negotiated and finalised through the Sport Endorse platform
  • Creator payments coordinated and processed through Sport Endorse
  • Campaign communication, approvals and content delivery managed through a single platform workflow
