BBC Sport NI × Nichola Fryday — Women's Rugby World Cup Punditry

The campaign

For the Women's Rugby World Cup 2025, BBC Sport NI partnered with former Ireland Rugby captain Nichola Fryday to deliver live punditry and analysis. Nichola drew on her international playing experience to give audiences authentic, expert insight, adding depth to BBC Sport NI's coverage.
