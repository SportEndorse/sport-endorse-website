AYA Supplements × Kerry Ladies — Official Team Partner

AYA Supplements became Kerry Senior Ladies' official 2024 partner, combining tailored nutrition support with fan-facing promotions and digital activation.

Brand ambassadorGaelic FootballIreland

Overview

The campaign

AYA Supplements partnered with Sport Endorse to become the official brand partner of the Kerry Senior Ladies for the 2024 season. The programme combined team-wide nutritional support, tailored supplementation with coaching and nutrition staff, and planned fan-facing promotions, events and digital content around one of Ireland's leading women's Gaelic football teams.

The challenge

AYA Supplements wanted a credible route into elite women's sport that connected its nutrition range with real performance, recovery and wellbeing needs. A simple logo placement would not have been enough. The brand needed a partnership with an established team, a clear performance rationale and a structure that could support both athlete use and wider consumer engagement throughout the season.

The challenge was also operational. Team partnerships involve multiple stakeholders, including management, coaching staff, nutrition personnel, commercial contacts and athletes. The activation therefore required a partner able to broker the relationship, align expectations and create a programme that made sense both to AYA as a healthcare and nutrition brand and to Kerry's high-performance environment.

The Sport Endorse solution

Sport Endorse brokered the partnership between AYA Supplements and the Kerry Senior Ladies for the 2024 season, securing official brand-partner status for AYA. The public partnership announcement confirmed that AYA would provide nutritional products formulated to support peak performance, recovery and overall wellbeing, while working with the team's coaching and nutrition staff to tailor supplementation strategies to athlete needs.

The partnership was designed to operate beyond the dressing room. AYA also committed to engaging Kerry supporters through product promotions, fan events and digital content, giving the collaboration both a performance-support layer and a consumer-facing activation layer. This created a regional platform through which the brand could build relevance with athletes, supporters and the wider women's-sport audience.

Internal HubSpot records independently confirm the commercial relationship. The Deal "AYA Supplements partner Kerry Senior Ladies" was created on 4 January 2024, owned by Trevor Twamley and associated with Aine Devlin and Uniphar. The CRM record does not contain additional campaign-performance metrics, and no matching opportunity was found in the Sport Endorse Admin Panel, so unverified reach or engagement figures have not been added.

Athlete fit

Kerry Senior Ladies offered AYA a particularly strong fit because the partnership placed a pharmacy-focused nutrition brand inside an elite team environment where recovery, preparation and sustained performance were everyday priorities. Rather than relying on a single influencer, the brand could support a full senior squad and work with the people responsible for athlete preparation.

The timing also gave the partnership strong sporting context. Kerry entered 2024 after back-to-back All-Ireland final appearances and later won the TG4 All-Ireland Senior Championship in August, securing the county's first senior title since 1993. That championship success should not be attributed to the sponsorship, but it demonstrates the elite competitive environment in which the AYA partnership operated.
