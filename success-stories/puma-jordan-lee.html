Puma Running × Jordan Lee — International Brand Ambassador

Puma Running appointed Irish Paralympian Jordan Lee as an international long-term ambassador, aligning elite para athletics with global running performance.

Brand ambassadorParalympicMulti-market

Overview

The campaign

Puma Running partnered with Sport Endorse to identify Irish Paralympian Jordan Lee for a long-term international ambassador role. A high jump and long jump specialist, Lee was announced as an Official International Puma Athlete ahead of the 2023 World Para Athletics Championships, giving the brand an authentic elite-performance partnership with international relevance.

The challenge

The available Sport Endorse success story states that Puma Running was looking for an international athlete to become a long-term brand ambassador. This was more demanding than a short social activation: the selected athlete needed to represent the brand over time, carry genuine elite-sport credibility and remain relevant across more than one market.

A long-term appointment also required a convincing connection between athlete and product category. Puma Running needed a profile associated with training, competition, resilience and measurable sporting progression, rather than a personality whose relevance depended only on audience size. The partnership also had to feel appropriate within international performance sport and give the brand a credible story around inclusion and para athletics without reducing the athlete to a symbolic role.

The Sport Endorse solution

Sport Endorse connected Puma Running with Jordan Lee, an elite Irish Paralympian specialising in high jump and long jump. The public success story confirms that Lee was selected as an Official International Puma Athlete and presents the relationship as a long-term ambassador appointment.

The selection gave Puma a single athlete profile with several useful points of alignment. Lee represented elite competition, Irish sport, para athletics and two technical jumping disciplines. His positioning ahead of the 2023 World Para Athletics Championships also placed the partnership within a recognised international sporting context, giving the announcement relevance beyond a one-off product post.

No matching HubSpot Deal was found during the internal review, so the case study does not claim unsupported details about contracting dates, content volumes, product allocations, campaign markets or platform-specific deliverables. Instead, it focuses on the verified outcome: Sport Endorse helped Puma Running secure an international athlete for an ongoing ambassador relationship, matching the requirement stated in the original public brief.

Athlete fit

Jordan Lee was a strong fit because his sporting identity closely matched Puma Running's performance positioning. As an elite Irish Paralympian, he brought high-performance credibility and an established connection to international competition. His specialisms in high jump and long jump also demonstrated speed, power, technique and disciplined preparation, attributes that translate naturally into a performance-sports brand partnership.

The selection also broadened the ambassador story beyond conventional road running. Lee gave Puma an association with para athletics and field-event performance while remaining directly connected to running mechanics, explosive movement and track competition. Most importantly, the available source presents him as an athlete selected for official international status, not simply as a short-term content creator. That distinction supports the long-term nature of the brief and makes the partnership more credible.

Deliverables

  • One international athlete, Jordan Lee, selected for the Puma Running ambassador requirement.
  • Jordan Lee publicly announced as an Official International Puma Athlete.
  • Long-term brand ambassador positioning confirmed in the Sport Endorse success story.
  • Partnership connected to elite Irish para athletics and international competition.
  • Athlete profile represented two disciplines: high jump and long jump.
  • Ambassador announcement positioned in the context of the 2023 World Para Athletics Championships.

Results

The verified record confirms one international athlete appointment, two athletics disciplines and one long-term ambassador relationship. It also links the announcement to the 2023 World Para Athletics Championships. Reach, impressions, engagement, sales impact, contract duration and the internal sourcing timeline were not available, so no performance figures have been inferred.
