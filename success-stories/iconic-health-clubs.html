Iconic Health Clubs × Irish Rugby Stars — Community Event

Iconic Health Clubs partnered with Sport Endorse to create an exclusive rugby community event featuring leading Irish rugby personalities for members and their families.

Speaking & eventsRugbyIreland

Overview

The campaign

Iconic Health Clubs partnered with Sport Endorse to strengthen member engagement by bringing leading Irish rugby personalities to an exclusive community event hosted by Alan Quinlan. Sport Endorse managed athlete sourcing, coordination and event logistics, delivering a seamless experience for members and their families.

The challenge

Iconic Health Clubs wanted to offer members something beyond a traditional fitness experience. The objective was to create an exclusive community event that rewarded members with access to respected figures from Irish rugby while strengthening relationships within the club.

Securing high-profile sports personalities, coordinating schedules and managing event logistics can be time-consuming and complex, particularly for organisations without dedicated talent management resources. Iconic Health Clubs needed an experienced partner who could simplify the entire process and ensure the event ran smoothly from planning through to delivery.

The Sport Endorse solution

Sport Endorse worked closely with Iconic Health Clubs to source suitable Irish rugby personalities and coordinate every stage of the event.

Hosted by former Irish international Alan Quinlan at Iconic Health Clubs' Dartry location, the evening brought together members, families and rugby fans for an engaging programme of stories, discussion and audience Q&A.

Throughout the planning process, Sport Endorse provided ongoing guidance, coordinated talent logistics and remained closely involved to ensure the client received support at every stage. The result was a professionally managed event that delivered a memorable experience for attendees while allowing the Iconic Health Clubs team to focus on hosting their members.

Athlete fit

Irish rugby enjoys broad appeal across Ireland, making it a natural fit for a community-focused health club.

By partnering with respected rugby personalities, Iconic Health Clubs created an authentic experience that resonated with both adult members and younger rugby fans. Alan Quinlan's experience as both a former international player and established broadcaster made him an ideal host to facilitate conversation, engage the audience and create an enjoyable atmosphere throughout the evening.

Deliverables

  • Live community event hosted at Iconic Health Clubs Dartry
  • Event hosted by Alan Quinlan
  • Live rugby discussion featuring Irish rugby personalities
  • Audience Q&A session
