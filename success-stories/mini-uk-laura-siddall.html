MINI UK × Laura Siddall — Friend of the Brand

Sport Endorse connected MINI UK with professional triathlete Laura Siddall to create an authentic ambassador partnership built on genuine brand affinity and active lifestyle credibility.

Brand ambassadorTriathlonUK

Overview

The campaign

Laura Siddall, a professional triathlete with an engineering degree and a former officer in the British Army, partnered with Sport Endorse due to her being passionate about wanting to work with MINI UK. Laura views the MINI brand as quintessentially British, with outstanding design and a tremendous heritage. As we always say, when an athlete is deeply passionate about a brand it can be a great fit, so we reached out to MINI UK.

The challenge

Laura Siddall had a clear ambition: to work with MINI UK. As a professional triathlete, former British Army officer, and engineering graduate, she had been a genuine admirer of the brand for many years. She identified strongly with MINI's British heritage, engineering excellence, distinctive design, and adventurous spirit, making it one of the few brands she actively wanted to represent.

However, like many elite athletes, Laura faced a common sponsorship challenge. Despite being an established professional with an engaged audience and strong personal brand, identifying the right decision-makers within a major automotive company-and securing meaningful conversations-can be difficult, time-consuming, and often relies on existing industry connections.

Rather than pursuing a broad sponsorship search, Laura wanted to build a partnership with a brand she already believed in. The challenge was turning that genuine enthusiasm into a commercial relationship that would deliver value for both Laura and MINI UK.

The Sport Endorse solution

Through Sport Endorse, Laura shared her interest in working with MINI UK, recognising the brand as an authentic fit with her personal values and lifestyle. Sport Endorse identified the opportunity, approached MINI UK on Laura's behalf, and introduced a partnership built on genuine brand affinity rather than a conventional sponsorship proposal.

The authenticity of Laura's connection with the brand became the foundation of the relationship. After discussions with MINI UK, Laura was appointed as a Friend of the Brand, creating an ambassador partnership that allowed her to naturally incorporate the MINI Countryman into her training, travel, and everyday life.

Sport Endorse managed the introduction and facilitated the relationship, enabling both athlete and brand to establish a partnership based on shared values. The collaboration extended beyond product use, with Laura supporting MINI UK's lifestyle and community initiatives, including its involvement with parkrun, while creating authentic content that resonated with endurance athletes and active consumers.

Deliverables

  • Athlete-brand matching through the Sport Endorse platform
  • Introduction and partnership facilitation between Laura Siddall and MINI UK
  • Appointment as MINI UK Friend of the Brand
  • Ongoing ambassador relationship
  • Lifestyle content featuring the MINI Countryman
  • Product integration demonstrating real-world vehicle practicality
  • Athlete-generated social media content
  • Brand storytelling centred on British heritage and engineering
  • Participation in MINI UK's community fitness and parkrun initiatives
  • The Trusted Quote
  • "I've been a long-time fan of MINI. It's a brand that for me is so British and carries this heritage through its design over the years. My favourite film is the 1969 The Italian Job, and how can you not love this brand and the cars? I was delighted when, through Sport Endorse, I became a Friend of the Brand for MINI UK. I'm lucky enough to be driving a MINI Countryman, and even better, it fits my TT bike in the boot without needing to take any wheels off."
  • Laura Siddall | Professional Triathlete
