Hard Rock Cafe Dublin × Kaci Rock — Brunch-Menu Launch

Hard Rock Cafe Dublin partnered with Sport Endorse to launch its new brunch menu through authentic athlete-created social content, generating over 35,000 Instagram views.

Campaign activationBoxingIreland

Overview

The campaign

Hard Rock Cafe Dublin partnered with Sport Endorse to generate local awareness and drive engagement for the launch of its new brunch menu by connecting directly with Irish boxer Kaci Rock. Managed seamlessly through our digital platform.

The challenge

Hard Rock Cafe Dublin wanted to promote the launch of its new weekend brunch menu through authentic local content rather than traditional advertising. The brand needed athletes who were comfortable creating lifestyle-focused content, had genuine influence within the Irish market, and could communicate naturally with their audiences.

Managing athlete outreach manually would have required identifying suitable creators, negotiating collaborations individually, and coordinating campaign deliverables across multiple conversations. Hard Rock Cafe instead wanted a streamlined solution that could efficiently source relevant talent while keeping campaign management simple.

The Sport Endorse solution

Using the Sport Endorse platform, Hard Rock Cafe Dublin launched a targeted campaign inviting Irish athletes to apply for the opportunity.

Within the platform, the brand reviewed applications, compared athlete profiles, managed conversations, and selected the most suitable creators. Two athlete collaborations were confirmed, with Irish boxer Kaci Rock becoming the featured campaign ambassador.

Each selected athlete received a hosted brunch experience, drinks, merchandise, and campaign compensation in exchange for agreed Instagram content. The entire sourcing, communication and contracting process was managed through Sport Endorse's digital platform.

Athlete fit

Kaci Rock was a strong match for the campaign because she combines elite sporting credibility with an authentic and engaging social media presence. As a European boxing medallist and one of Ireland's emerging female athletes, she regularly shares lifestyle content alongside her sporting journey, making a restaurant experience feel genuine rather than overly promotional.

Her approachable style allowed Hard Rock Cafe Dublin to showcase the new brunch menu through trusted first-hand recommendations, helping the campaign resonate with a local audience.

Deliverables

  • Campaign brief published through the Sport Endorse platform
  • 30 athletes sourced through the platform
  • 2 athletes selected and contracted
  • Hosted brunch experience for selected athletes
  • Instagram Post and/or Reel
  • Instagram Stories
  • Campaign communication and payment managed through Sport Endorse
