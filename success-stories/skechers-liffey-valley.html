Skechers Ireland × Athletes — Store-Opening Activation

Skechers Ireland activated athletes in-store to showcase its retail experience and promote an exclusive 20% weekend offer through social content.

Campaign activationMultipleIreland

Overview

The campaign

Skechers Ireland used Sport Endorse to connect with a selection of well-known athletes for a local retail activation. Athletes visited the refurbished Liffey Valley store, selected footwear and clothing, and created social content promoting an exclusive 20% weekend discount, combining product experience, physical retail and trusted sporting influence.

The challenge

Skechers Ireland wanted to generate attention around its Dublin retail presence and give consumers a clear reason to visit during a promotional weekend. Retail offers can disappear quickly in crowded social feeds, so the brand needed credible local personalities who could turn a discount into an engaging store experience and present the products through a recognisable human perspective.

The original Sport Endorse directory headline references the Liffey Valley and Tallaght stores and a 20% opening offer. The detailed legacy copy specifically documents the refurbished Liffey Valley Shopping Centre location. The campaign therefore supported wider Dublin store messaging, while the surviving execution details relate to an in-person activation at Liffey Valley. This case study keeps that distinction clear and does not assume unverified activity at Tallaght.

The Sport Endorse solution

Sport Endorse connected Skechers Ireland with a selection of well-known athletes from its platform. Rather than promoting the discount remotely, the athletes visited the Liffey Valley store during a specified weekend, explored the refurbished space and chose products suited to their preferences and active lifestyles.

Each athlete received a pair of shoes and a selection of clothing. In return, they created social media content featuring the store experience and communicating the exclusive 20% discount. The arrangement gave the promotion more substance than a standard offer post, showing recognised sporting personalities interacting naturally with the brand and sharing a timely reason to visit.

The activation combined regional relevance, product seeding and social amplification. Sport Endorse managed the connection with suitable athletes, while the store visit created a natural content setting. Searches for Skechers, Shuz Group, Liffey Valley, Tallaght and the 20% offer did not locate a relevant 2022 or 2023 HubSpot Deal. A later record concerned proposed 2024 activity and was excluded, making the published legacy story the primary verified source.

Athlete fit

Recognised athletes were a strong fit because footwear and sportswear can be demonstrated visually and personally. Their association with movement, comfort and active routines was relevant without requiring technical performance claims. Allowing each participant to select suitable items created a more believable connection between athlete, product and store experience.

Using several athletes broadened the campaign's local relevance and enabled Skechers Ireland to reach different sporting communities. Their recognition helped turn a national footwear brand into a Dublin retail moment, while social content extended the activation beyond shoppers already in the centre. The participating athletes' names and sports were not preserved, so this case study describes the verified group without guessing the roster.

Deliverables

  • The published legacy record confirms the following campaign structure and deliverables:
  • A selection of well-known athletes sourced and connected with Skechers Ireland through Sport Endorse.
  • In-person athlete visits to the newly refurbished Skechers store in Liffey Valley Shopping Centre.
  • Product seeding consisting of each athlete's choice of shoes and a selection of clothing.
  • Athlete-created social media content featuring the store experience and selected products.
  • Promotion of an exclusive 20% discount available during the specified campaign weekend.
  • A regional retail activation supporting Skechers Ireland's Liffey Valley and Tallaght store messaging.

Results

The evidence confirms one documented in-store activation weekend, an exclusive 20% offer and two seeded product categories: footwear and clothing. The directory headline references Liffey Valley and Tallaght, while the detailed execution copy verifies the athlete visit at Liffey Valley. The athlete count and names, post volume, formats, platforms, reach, engagement, store footfall, redemptions and sales uplift were not preserved and have not been estimated.
