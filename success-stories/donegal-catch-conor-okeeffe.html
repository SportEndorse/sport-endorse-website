Donegal Catch (Green Isle) × Conor O'Keeffe — Content Campaign

Donegal Catch partnered with endurance athlete Conor O'Keeffe to turn simple fish preparation and nutrition benefits into authentic social content.

Social campaignAthleticsIreland

Overview

The campaign

Donegal Catch and Green Isle Foods used Sport Endorse to partner with Irish ultra-endurance coach Conor O'Keeffe in 2023. The campaign translated simple fish preparation, taste and nutritional value into credible athlete-led social content, connecting a familiar food brand with consumers interested in active lifestyles and practical everyday meals.

The challenge

Donegal Catch wanted to show that fish could be straightforward to prepare, enjoyable to eat and relevant to an active lifestyle. Those messages are useful, but they can easily become generic when delivered only through product packaging or conventional recipe advertising. Green Isle Foods therefore needed a sporting voice who could connect convenience and nutrition with a real routine, while still presenting the food in an accessible way for ordinary households.

The activation also required the right balance between performance credibility and broad consumer appeal. The selected athlete needed to be recognised for discipline, training and wellbeing, but the content could not feel designed only for elite competitors. It had to make the product approachable for people seeking practical meal ideas, including consumers with busy schedules. The challenge was to demonstrate preparation and eating occasions naturally, communicate nutritional benefits responsibly and avoid turning the partnership into a forced endorsement.

The Sport Endorse solution

Sport Endorse connected Donegal Catch with Conor O'Keeffe, an Irish ultra-endurance athlete and coach whose public profile is closely associated with preparation, resilience and sustained performance. That background gave the campaign a credible route into nutrition-led storytelling. Rather than relying on celebrity visibility alone, the partnership used an athlete whose everyday training environment made food choice and recovery genuinely relevant.

The legacy Sport Endorse success story identifies Conor as the sports influencer selected to demonstrate how easy and tasty Donegal Catch fish is to cook and eat, while also highlighting its nutritional benefits. This gave the campaign a clear content framework: show the product in use, keep the preparation understandable and connect the meal with an active routine. The approach allowed Donegal Catch to communicate practical value through a recognisable person rather than through abstract claims.

Internal HubSpot records confirm that the collaboration was managed as a completed 2023 opportunity. Two duplicate Deals titled "Conor O'Keeffe Donegal Catch" were located, created on 23 February and 6 March 2023. Both carry the same close date of 30 August 2023, the stage Opportunity Completed, High Priority status and ownership by Jorge Ruiz de Palacios. The duplicate records do not preserve emails, notes, platform-level publishing details or performance metrics, so they are treated as supporting evidence for one completed campaign rather than two separate activations.

Athlete fit

Conor was a strong fit because endurance sport makes preparation, energy and recovery visible parts of the athlete story. As an ultra-endurance coach, he could discuss food within a context where consistency and practical choices matter. That gave Donegal Catch a credible spokesperson for nutritional relevance without requiring exaggerated performance claims or an artificial connection between the athlete and the product.

His fit also extended beyond elite sport. Endurance content often focuses on routines, planning and manageable habits, themes that translate well to consumers who simply want convenient and balanced meal options. By showing fish preparation through an athlete lens, the campaign could make the product feel both performance-aware and suitable for everyday family use. Conor's Irish profile also matched the brand's domestic market and supported a culturally relevant partnership for Green Isle Foods.

Deliverables

  • The verified records confirm the following campaign structure and deliverables:
  • One athlete partnership activated with Irish ultra-endurance coach Conor O'Keeffe.
  • Athlete-led social content developed around Donegal Catch fish preparation and consumption.
  • Campaign messaging focused on ease of cooking, taste and nutritional benefits.
  • Product storytelling connected with training, wellbeing and an active everyday routine.
  • One completed 2023 Sport Endorse opportunity documented in two duplicate HubSpot records.
  • Brand and athlete coordination managed through Sport Endorse for the Irish market.

Results

The evidence confirms one activated athlete, one completed campaign and three core communication themes: easy preparation, enjoyable taste and nutritional value. The earliest duplicate Deal was created on 23 February and both records closed on 30 August 2023, giving a documented CRM window of 188 days. Verified post counts, formats, social platforms, reach, impressions, views, engagement rate, clicks, sales and conversion data were not available in the records reviewed, so none have been estimated.
