HoverAir × 14 Athletes — Pan-European Brand Awareness

HOVERAir partnered with Sport Endorse to scale European brand awareness through authentic athlete-generated content showcasing its AI-powered flying camera across multiple sports.

Social campaignMultipleEurope

Overview

The campaign

HoverAir partnered with Sport Endorse to expand brand awareness across Europe by connecting directly with European athletes and outdoor content creators across cycling, running, snow sports, mountaineering and travel. Managed seamlessly through our digital platform.

The challenge

As HOVERAir prepared to grow its presence across Europe, the brand needed an efficient way to showcase its AI-powered flying camera in authentic outdoor environments. Rather than relying on traditional advertising, HOVERAir wanted credible creators who could naturally demonstrate the product while participating in activities such as cycling, running, snow sports and mountaineering.

Managing this type of campaign manually would have required sourcing suitable athletes across multiple countries, reviewing applications individually, coordinating product shipments, negotiating deliverables, managing approvals and tracking campaign progress-creating significant administrative overhead for an international marketing team.

The Sport Endorse solution

Using the Sport Endorse platform, HoverAir launched a multi-market campaign targeting European athletes and outdoor creators with a minimum social following of 30,000.

The platform enabled the HoverAir team to:

Publish a detailed campaign brief.

Receive and review athlete applications in one central dashboard.

Compare applicants based on audience, sport and content style.

Select creators for product seeding.

Manage communication throughout the collaboration.

Coordinate content approval before publication.

Deliver products in exchange for agreed content deliverables.

Rather than paying traditional sponsorship fees, HoverAir adopted a value-in-kind product seeding model, providing each selected creator with a HOVERAir X1 ProMax package valued at approximately €1,200.

Athlete fit

The selected athletes represented sports where dynamic outdoor footage naturally enhances storytelling. Cyclists, runners, snowboarders, mountain athletes and travel creators were able to demonstrate the HoverAir ProMax exactly as consumers would use it-capturing hands-free footage while remaining fully engaged in their activity.

This authentic use of the product helped position HoverAir as a practical filming solution rather than simply another consumer drone. By allowing athletes to create content in their own style, the campaign generated genuine product demonstrations instead of scripted advertisements, increasing credibility among active, technology-conscious audiences across Europe.

Deliverables

  • The campaign included a structured multi-platform content package for each selected creator:
  • Campaign brief published to targeted European athletes.
  • 58 verified athlete applications received through the Sport Endorse platform.
  • 23 athlete collaborations confirmed through the platform.
  • HOVERAir X1 ProMax, Beacon, Joystick and accessories supplied (approximate value: €1,200 per creator).
  • One Instagram Reel featuring HOVERAir product integration.
  • One Instagram Story promoting the Reel.
  • One TikTok featuring HOVERAir product integration.
  • One YouTube integration of at least 10 seconds showcasing the product or captured footage.
  • Brand review and approval completed before content publication.
  • Athlete tagging guidelines provided for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.
